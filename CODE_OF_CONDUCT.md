# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Discuss code and ideas without personal attacks.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, spam and disclosure of
another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Give constructive feedback and respect requests to stop disruptive behavior. Maintainers
may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, restrict participation or block accounts.
Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact.

Report concerns privately to crossingguarddev@gmail.com. Include relevant context without
posting sensitive details publicly. Reports will be handled as privately as practical;
absolute confidentiality and a fixed response time are not promised.
